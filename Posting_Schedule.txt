Fuel is pumped up from an underground tank through a flow meter. In many pumps, pistons fill and empty fixed chambers, so each stroke is a known volume. The meter shaft triggers electrical pulses.

How Planes Stay Up in the Sky ✈️ #shorts

YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION:
Engines push the plane forward fast, around 250 km/h for take-off. The curved, tilted wings deflect huge amounts of air downward, and the air pushes the wing up (Newton's third law).

Air also speeds up over the curved top, lowering pressure there. That difference is lift. Flaps slide out for take-off and landing to give extra lift at low speed.
